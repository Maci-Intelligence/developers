# Example questions

The assistant answers from what the tools give, so the more precise the question, the better.

## Overview

- What units do we have, by site?
- Which of our units have R404A, and how much in total?
- Which units have no refrigerant or charge registered?
- What changed in our units this week?

## Leak checks and F-gas

- Which units are due a leak check in the next three months? Overdue first.
- Which units are covered by leak checks but have never been checked in Maci?
- Show the F-log of MC-0482 for this year.
- What is the CO2 equivalent of the refrigerant at each site?

## Alarms

- Are there open alarms right now? Who has taken them?
- How many alarms did we have last month, and how many were false?
- Which units had the most alarms in the last 90 days?
- How long did it take, on average, before an alarm was taken?

## Service

- When was each cold room at the main store last serviced?
- What has been done on MC-0482 in the last two years?
- Which components does MC-0482 have, and when were they installed?

## For a refrigeration company

- Which customers' units are due a leak check this month?
- Give me a list of open alarms at all our customers, most urgent first.
